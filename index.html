What Consistency Rules Actually Cost You

Let's be honest about what's really happening. Consistency rules sound reasonable on paper. In practice, they destroy the edge of every trader who doesn't produce perfectly smooth daily returns — which is almost everyone.

💥

Your best day gets capped — you nail a perfect setup, bank $800, and now you need 4 more mediocre days just to make the distribution look "even"

Consistency rules don't make you a better trader. They make you a worse version of yourself trying to fit inside someone else's model.
